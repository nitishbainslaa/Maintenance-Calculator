Maintenance Calculator Under Indian Law

The Supreme Court in Rajnesh v. Neha introduced uniform guidelines for determining maintenance in matrimonial disputes. Courts consider income, liabilities, dependents, lifestyle and child expenses while deciding maintenance.

How Maintenance Is Calculated In India

Maintenance depends upon income, liabilities, marriage duration, standard of living, children and earning capacity of both spouses.
